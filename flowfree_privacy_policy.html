Privacy at a glance: Flow Free does not require an account or login. The game can be played offline, and your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Flow Free does not require registration or login and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The core game can be played offline. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a personal account or cloud database for your game data.
